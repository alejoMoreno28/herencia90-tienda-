## Interface Direction

- Prefer fewer words. The product photo, title, price, and one clear action should carry most cards.
- Avoid repeated badges on every product. If everything says "Top" or "15 dias", nothing feels special.
- Keep sections direct: "Populares", "Clubes", "Selecciones", "Joyas Retro".
- Use cards only for actual product items. Sections should feel like clean catalog rows, not boxes inside boxes.
- Prioritize iconic and visually strong shirts first: Barcelona 2008/09, Milan 2006/07, Argentina 1994/2006, Arsenal classics, Manchester United 90s/00s, Liverpool 2004/05, Brasil 2002, Colombia 1990, River/Boca icons.
- Motion should be quick and useful: hover lift, press feedback, and image swap. Avoid heavy decorative animation.
- Buttons and product cards should use compact radii, restrained borders, and clear active states.
